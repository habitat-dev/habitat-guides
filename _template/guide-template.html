Payments, cancellations + insurance

Deposit + balance

Your $500 deposit is paid and your spot is held. Your full balance is due by {{BALANCE_DUE_DATE}} (30 days before the retreat), following the payment plan you chose at checkout. Payments come out automatically on the card saved at checkout and show on your statement as HABITAT RETREATS.

If you need to cancel

Life happens. Here's how it works (full details in our Terms + Conditions):

If you cancelWhat happens
On or before {{DATE_140}}
(140+ days before)		Your payments (not your deposit) can be refunded, or moved to another Habitat retreat within 12 months
{{DATE_139}} to {{DATE_91}}
(139 to 91 days before)		50% of the retreat total (not counting your deposit) is due and non-refundable. If you've paid less than that, the difference is charged at this time
{{DATE_90}} or later
(90 days or less)		No refunds. 100% of the retreat total is due at this time
